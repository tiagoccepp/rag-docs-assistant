# Northwind Labs — Expense & Travel Policy

Effective January 2026. Applies to all employees and contractors.

## Expense Limits

No approval needed under **USD 50**. Manager approval required from USD 50 to USD 500.
Finance approval required above **USD 500**. All expenses must be submitted in Ramp
within 30 days with an itemized receipt.

## Travel

- Flights under 6 hours: economy class. Flights 6+ hours: premium economy allowed.
- Hotel cap is **USD 180 per night** (USD 250 in San Francisco, London, and Tokyo).
- Daily meal allowance is **USD 60**; alcohol is not reimbursable.
- Book travel at least 14 days in advance unless approved as urgent.

## Equipment

New hires receive a MacBook Pro 14, monitor up to USD 400, and accessories up to
USD 200. Replacements are covered after 3 years or on documented failure. Lost or
stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Software

Approved tools (Notion, Figma, GitHub, Linear) are expensed centrally — do not buy
seats yourself. New tool requests above USD 20/month need manager approval via the
#procurement Slack channel. Annual contracts above USD 5,000 need Finance sign-off.

## Consultants

Contractor day rates above USD 800 require VP approval. All consulting agreements must
use the standard MSA template and be countersigned before work begins.
