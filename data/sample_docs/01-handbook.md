# Northwind Labs — Employee Handbook

Welcome to Northwind Labs! This handbook summarizes how we work. Last updated March 2026.

## Working Hours

Our core collaboration hours are **10:00 to 15:00 America/Montevideo**, Monday to Friday.
Outside core hours you may arrange a flexible schedule with your manager.
The standard work week is 40 hours. Overtime is paid at 1.5x for hours above 40 in a week.

## Remote Work

Northwind is a **remote-first** company. You may work from anywhere with at least 4 hours
of overlap with core hours. We provide a **USD 150 monthly stipend** for coworking or home
office expenses. Reimbursements are processed on the 5th of each month via Deel.

## Time Off

- Full-time employees receive **20 vacation days per year**, accrued monthly.
- Sick leave is **unlimited** but requires notice to your manager before 10:00 on the day.
- Parental leave is **12 weeks fully paid** for any new parent.
- Uruguay public holidays are observed; staff in other countries observe their local holidays.

## Onboarding

Your first week includes: a buddy assignment, security training, and a 30-minute welcome
call with the CEO. Laptops (MacBook Pro 14) ship before day one. Access provisioning is
handled through Okta and typically completes within 24 hours of contract signature.

## Performance Reviews

Reviews happen **twice a year** (April and October). Ratings are: Exceeds, Meets,
Partially Meets, Does Not Meet. Promotions require two consecutive Exceeds ratings and
a written growth plan approved by your manager and skip-level.

## Code of Conduct

We have zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination. Report concerns to
people@northwindlabs.example or through the anonymous form. All reports are
acknowledged within 48 hours and investigated confidentially.
